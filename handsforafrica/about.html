● Who we are

We are Hands for Africa.

A team working on the ground in Mozambique so that no child grows up hungry, sick, or without a school.

Our story

Hands for Africa began in 2014 in a single classroom in Nampula, northern Mozambique, where a handful of teachers and nurses refused to watch children go hungry. What started as one daily meal for forty children has grown into food, health and education programs across 23 communities.

We are not a distant office sending money abroad. Our teams live and work where the children do. Every meal, every vaccine, every school book is delivered by our own people on the ground.

Our mission

Our mission is simple: that every child in the communities we serve wakes up fed, healthy, and able to go to school. No middlemen. No excuses.

What we do

Food

Hot meals every day through community kitchens, so hunger never keeps a child from learning or growing.

Health

Vaccines, checkups and clean water that stop preventable disease before it takes a life.

Education

School supplies, scholarships and support for local teachers, so a child's future is decided by their potential, not their poverty.

Where we work

We work across northern and central Mozambique, in the provinces of Nampula, Zambézia and Cabo Delgado, partnering with local schools, clinics and community leaders. Our coordination office is in Maputo.

What we believe

We believe in dawn, not pity. We show children as they are: full of life, laughter and potential. We treat every family with dignity, and we measure our work by the futures we help build.
