Who we are ✦

For years, Hands for Africa has reached the children the world forgot, working on the ground with schools, community kitchens and health centers. No middlemen. No excuses.

We give them far more than food and medicine. We give them presence, safety, and the chance to be children again.

Because no child should have to fight to survive alone. And with you beside us, none of them will.
